## About Laravel

Laravel is a web application framework with expressive, elegant syntax. We believe development must be an enjoyable and creative experience to be truly fulfilling. Laravel takes the pain out of development by easing common tasks used in many web projects, such as:

- [Simple, fast routing engine](https://laravel.com/docs/routing).
- [Powerful dependency injection container](https://laravel.com/docs/container).
- Multiple back-ends for [session](https://laravel.com/docs/session) and [cache](https://laravel.com/docs/cache) storage.
- Expressive, intuitive [database ORM](https://laravel.com/docs/eloquent).
- Database agnostic [schema migrations](https://laravel.com/docs/migrations).
- [Robust background job processing](https://laravel.com/docs/queues).
- [Real-time event broadcasting](https://laravel.com/docs/broadcasting).

Laravel is accessible, powerful, and provides tools required for large, robust applications.

# SIA 2 Activities

## Activity 1

Activity 1 focuses on creating a Food and Beverage Menu that showcases different meals and drinks with their corresponding categories, prices, and short descriptions to provide customers with clear and organized menu information.

## Activity 2

Activity 2 focuses on developing a Food Menu System that allows users to browse different menu items digitally, making menu viewing faster, easier, and more convenient for customers and administrators.

## Activity 3

Activity 3 focuses on creating a Student Form System that allows users to input, manage, and organize student information efficiently and accurately.

## Activity 4

Activity 4 focuses on creating UML Diagrams for the FOOD MENU RESTAURANT System to visually represent the interaction between users and the system, including customer and admin functions such as managing menu items, placing orders, and processing payments.

## Activity 5

Activity 5 focuses on developing a News Dashboard using Laravel that integrates authentication, APIs, and external data sources to display news information dynamically for users through an organized and interactive dashboard.



# APPLICATION DEVELOPMENT

## Activity 1

MediLink PH is a smart healthcare monitoring and appointment system proposed by Maria Theresa Penaso (BSIT 3-B). Designed to tackle healthcare accessibility challenges, long wait times, and manual record-keeping in communities like Southern Leyte, the system leverages Artificial Intelligence (AI) and Internet of Things (IoT) integration to digitize and streamline patient care.

## Activity 1(VIDEO_GROUPINGS)

Activity 1 focuses on The Internet of Things (IoT) refers to a vast network of physical objects—"things"—embedded with sensors, software, and other technologies to connect and exchange data with other devices and systems over the internet. By linking the physical and digital worlds, IoT transforms everyday objects into smart, data-driven tools.

## Activity 2
Activity 2 focuses on Food Ordering System, which is a digital platform designed to automate and simplify how a restaurant operates. It connects the front-end customer experience (ordering and paying) with the back-end kitchen and management operations
